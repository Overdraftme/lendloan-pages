What Is a Nurse or Healthcare Worker Home Loan?

While the full LMI waiver is typically reserved for doctors, many lenders offer partial LMI discounts, more flexible lending policies, and higher LVR options for registered nurses, midwives, paramedics, physiotherapists, and other allied health professionals. Lenders also often assess overtime, shift allowances, and penalty rates more generously — significantly boosting borrowing power.

Do nurses get the same LMI waiver as doctors?

Not always — the full LMI waiver is typically reserved for medical practitioners. However, many lenders offer partial LMI discounts for registered nurses and allied health workers.

This varies significantly by lender. We know which lenders are most generous for healthcare worker income structures.
