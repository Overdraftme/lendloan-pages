What Is an SMSF Property Loan (LRBA)?

An SMSF loan (formally a Limited Recourse Borrowing Arrangement) allows your self-managed super fund to borrow money to purchase an investment property. The property is held in a bare trust until the loan is repaid. Strict ATO compliance rules apply — getting the structure wrong has serious tax and legal consequences.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum SMSF balance to buy property?

Most advisers recommend a minimum fund balance of $200,000–$250,000 before purchasing property, to ensure sufficient liquidity for ongoing expenses and member benefit payments.

Can I buy a property from myself in my SMSF?

No — related-party transactions are heavily restricted under superannuation law. You cannot purchase residential property from a related party.

Which lenders still do SMSF loans?

The SMSF lending market has contracted significantly — most major banks have exited. We work with the remaining specialist and non-bank lenders active in this space.
