What Is a Bridging Loan?

A bridging loan bridges the financial gap between purchasing a new property and selling your existing one. During the bridging period (typically up to 12 months), you service interest on your peak debt — the combined balance of your existing loan and the new purchase. When your existing property sells, the proceeds pay down the peak debt and you're left with just your new home loan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 'peak debt' in a bridging loan?

Peak debt is the maximum amount you owe simultaneously — your existing loan balance plus the new purchase price including costs.

How long do I have to sell my existing property?

Most bridging loans allow 6–12 months to sell. We always recommend a conservative sale price estimate.

Are bridging loan rates higher than standard rates?

Typically slightly higher as they're short-term specialist products. However, most clients find bridging finance cost-effective when weighed against missing their ideal property.
