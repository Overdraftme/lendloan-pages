Saving a 20% deposit to buy in Sydney can take over a decade. But there's a Federal Government scheme that lets eligible first home buyers buy with just a 5% deposit — and pay no Lenders Mortgage Insurance at all. It's called the First Home Guarantee, and in 2026 it remains one of the most powerful tools available to Sydney first home buyers.

What Is the First Home Guarantee?

The First Home Guarantee allows eligible first home buyers to purchase with as little as a 5% deposit without paying LMI. The Federal Government guarantees the remaining 15% — eliminating LMI costs entirely.

On a $900,000 Sydney property, LMI would normally cost $20,000–$35,000. The First Home Guarantee eliminates this completely.

Eligibility Criteria 2026

  • Australian citizen or permanent resident aged 18 or over
  • First home buyer — never previously owned residential property in Australia
  • Individual income under $125,000 per year (or $200,000 combined for couples)
  • Purchasing a property to live in — investment properties are not eligible
  • Property price must be within the scheme's price caps (currently $900,000 in Greater Sydney)

How Much Can You Save?

Combined with the NSW First Home Owner Grant and stamp duty exemptions, eligible first home buyers can save $40,000–$76,000 in upfront costs:

  • 5% Deposit Scheme: saves $20,000–$35,000 in LMI
  • NSW First Home Owner Grant: $10,000 (new builds only)
  • Stamp duty exemption: up to $31,000 (properties under $800,000)

What Happens to the Government Guarantee After I Buy?

The government's 15% guarantee is released automatically once your LVR drops to 80% — through loan repayments or property value growth. You're not charged for the guarantee or required to pay it back.

Can I Combine the 5% Scheme with Other Grants?

Yes — if purchasing a new build you can combine the First Home Guarantee (no LMI) with the NSW First Home Owner Grant ($10,000) and potentially stamp duty exemptions. This is one of the most powerful first home buyer combinations available in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the 5% scheme on an existing property?

Yes — the First Home Guarantee applies to both new and established properties within the price cap. Unlike the FHOG, it's not restricted to new builds.

What happens to the government's guarantee after I buy?

The guarantee is released automatically once your LVR drops to 80%. You're not charged for the guarantee or required to pay it back.

Can I use the 5% scheme for an investment property?

No — the First Home Guarantee is only available for owner-occupied purchases.
