Common Questions

Everything you need to know

How much does a mortgage broker cost?
Our service is completely free for borrowers. We're paid by the lender after your loan settles — a standard industry practice. This means our advice costs you nothing, and we're incentivised to find you a loan that settles successfully. We compare over 50 lenders to ensure you're getting the best deal available.
How much can I borrow for a home loan?
Your borrowing capacity depends on your income, living expenses, existing debts, and the lender's serviceability criteria. As a general guide, most lenders allow you to borrow between 5 and 6 times your gross annual income. Use our free borrowing capacity calculator for an instant estimate, or call us for a personalised assessment based on your exact situation.
What grants are available for first home buyers in NSW?
NSW first home buyers can access the $10,000 First Home Owner Grant (for new homes up to $750,000), stamp duty exemptions on properties up to $800,000, and the First Home Guarantee scheme (buy with just 5% deposit, no LMI). We help every first home buyer client maximise the grants and concessions available to them.
How long does home loan approval take?
Pre-approval typically takes 1–3 business days. Full unconditional approval takes 5–10 business days depending on the lender and the complexity of your application. We manage the entire process, chase the assessors, and keep you updated at every milestone so there are no surprises.
Should I use a broker or go directly to a bank?
A bank can only offer you their own products. We compare over 50 lenders — including the big four banks, second-tier lenders, and specialist lenders — to find the loan that fits your situation. Our service is free, we do the paperwork, and we negotiate on your behalf. There's no downside to using a broker.
Do you help with refinancing?
Absolutely. Refinancing is one of our most popular services. If your current rate is above what's available in the market, we'll compare options across 50+ lenders and manage the entire switch. Many of our clients save $3,000–$8,000 per year simply by refinancing to a more competitive rate.
