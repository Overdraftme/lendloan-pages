What Is a Self-Employed Home Loan?

Self-employed borrowers are typically defined as individuals who have operated their own business, worked as a sole trader, or served as a company director for at least 12 months (some lenders require 2 years). The key is knowing which lenders are most generous with how they interpret self-employed income — full doc, low doc, or bank statement lending.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I need to have been self-employed?

Most standard lenders require 2 years. However, some specialist lenders will consider applicants with as little as 12 months ABN history, particularly if previously employed in the same industry.

What is a low doc loan?

A low documentation loan is designed for self-employed borrowers who can't document income through traditional means — using BAS statements, accountant's declaration, or bank statement lending instead.

My tax return shows a low income but my real earnings are much higher — what do I do?

Many lenders allow 'add-backs' — legitimate business expenses that reduce taxable income but don't reflect actual cash position. We work through your financials to maximise your assessable income.
