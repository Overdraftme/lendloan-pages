What Is a Doctor Home Loan?

Several of Australia's major lenders offer preferential home loan policies for registered medical professionals. These benefits typically include waiving Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) on loans up to 95% LVR — saving doctors $15,000–$40,000 upfront — plus higher borrowing capacity assessments and more flexible treatment of private practice income, overtime, and allowances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which medical professionals qualify for the LMI waiver?

Typically registered medical practitioners (GPs, specialists), dentists, pharmacists, physiotherapists, and certain allied health professionals. Eligibility varies by lender — we'll confirm in a free consultation.

How much can I borrow as a doctor?

Most lenders assess doctors generously — often up to 6–8 times annual income. With LMI waived and a higher LVR available, your purchasing power is significantly higher than a standard borrower.

I recently moved to private practice — can I still get a loan?

Yes. We specialise in complex medical income structures including the transition from PAYG to private practice.
